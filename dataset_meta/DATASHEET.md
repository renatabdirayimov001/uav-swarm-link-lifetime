# Datasheet: UAV Swarm Residual Link Lifetime Dataset (v2.1)

Following the structure of "Datasheets for Datasets" (Gebru et al.).

## Motivation
- **Purpose.** Benchmark the prediction of the residual lifetime of UAV-to-UAV links in flying ad hoc networks
  under a shadowed air-to-air (A2A) channel, using only information carried by Hello messages.
- **Creators.** Renat Abdirayimov (Tashkent University of Information Technologies named after Muhammad al-Khwarizmi).
- **Funding.** None.

## Composition
- **Instances.** One instance is one active link (i, j) in one slot t of one simulated episode.
  Features: positions and velocities of both UAVs with GNSS errors, measured SNR (with Rician fading),
  missed-Hello flag, LET, link state; labels: residual lifetime τ_ij(t) censored at H = 60 s and a censoring flag.
- **Size (main configurations, link shadowing model).** 6 configurations (RPGM / Gauss–Markov / random waypoint ×
  A2A / disk) × 23 episodes = 138 episodes of 600 s (1200 slots of 0.5 s), N = 30 UAVs (435 node pairs),
  11,046,489 active-link samples. Additional settings: motion-driven and spatially correlated shadowing
  (A2A, 3 × 23 episodes each).
- **Independent units.** The samples are *not* independent: consecutive slots of the same link are strongly
  correlated, and long-lived links contribute many samples (length-biased sampling). The independent units are the
  episodes; the natural event units are the link intervals and break events:

  | Mobility | Link model | Link intervals | Break events | Samples per interval |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | RPGM | A2A | 75,337 | 73,192 | 34.4 |
  | RPGM | Disk | 28,881 | 26,815 | 86.0 |
  | GM | A2A | 60,754 | 59,754 | 20.7 |
  | GM | Disk | 21,881 | 20,980 | 51.4 |
  | RWP | A2A | 80,648 | 79,127 | 23.5 |
  | RWP | Disk | 26,664 | 25,299 | 63.5 |

- **Splits.** By episode only: seeds 10000–10013 train, 10014–10016 validation, 10017–10022 test.
- **Missing data.** None; censored labels are marked explicitly. Every label has a full 60-s follow-up.
- **Sensitive data.** None (fully synthetic).

## Collection process
- Generated by `uav_swarm_sim.generate_episode(mobility, channel, seed, ...)` with `numpy.random.Generator` (PCG64).
- Channel parameters from published A2A measurement statistics (path-loss exponent 2.2, Rician K = 7.6 dB);
  the simulator is not fitted to recorded flight traces.

## Preprocessing
- No filtering. Features are scaled by fixed physical constants (range R, 35 m/s, 30 dB, area size), so no
  statistic of the test data is used for normalisation.

## Uses
- Intended: benchmarking link-lifetime / link-break predictors, censored regression and survival methods.
- Not intended: claims about real flight performance without validation on measured traces.

## Distribution and maintenance
- The dataset is distributed as a deterministic generator plus SHA-256 fingerprints of every canonical episode
  (`episode_sha256.csv`); `verify_dataset.py` regenerates and checks all episodes.
